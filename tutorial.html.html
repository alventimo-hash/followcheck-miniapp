How to use FollowCheck

FollowCheck shows you who doesn't follow you back on Instagram, and helps you unfollow them safely — all without ever uploading your data anywhere.

1

Export your Instagram data

In the Instagram app: go to your profile → tap the menu (≡) → Settings and privacy → Accounts Center → Your information and permissions → Export your information.

Choose Export to device → Some of your information → select Followers and following only (this keeps the file small). Set the date range to All time, then tap Start export.

2

Wait for the email

Instagram will email you a download link once your export is ready — this can take anywhere from a few minutes to a couple of days. Download the ZIP file to your phone when it arrives.

3

Open FollowCheck

Open this bot in Telegram and tap the menu button to launch FollowCheck.

4

Upload your export

Tap Choose File and select the ZIP file you downloaded from Instagram. FollowCheck reads and analyzes it instantly, right on your device — nothing is uploaded anywhere.

5

Review your results

You'll see how many accounts don't follow you back, your fans, mutuals, and a safe number to unfollow today based on your account's age and activity.

6

Unfollow safely

In the "Today's safe batch" list, tap Open → next to any account to open their Instagram profile directly. From there, tap Following → Unfollow yourself.

The safe batch limits how many you unfollow per day, to help protect your account from being flagged by Instagram for unusual activity.

7

Check back regularly

Repeat this process weekly (or whenever you like) with a fresh export to track how your followers change over time.
