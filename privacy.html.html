FollowCheck Privacy

What this app does

FollowCheck analyzes your Instagram data export (the ZIP file Instagram gives you when you request "Download your information") to show you who doesn't follow you back, your fans, mutuals, and a safe daily unfollow pace.

Where your data goes

Nowhere. All processing happens entirely on your own device, inside your browser. Your Instagram export file is never uploaded, transmitted, or sent to any server — not ours, not anyone else's.

What we store

To show you trends over time ("X new followers since last check"), the app saves a small snapshot of your follower list using your browser's local storage. This data stays on your device only and is never transmitted anywhere.

Third parties

The app loads one external code library (JSZip, for reading ZIP files) from a public code hosting service (cdnjs). No user data is sent to this service — it only provides the code that runs in your browser.

Your Instagram account

This app never logs into your Instagram account, never automates any actions on it, and never accesses Instagram's servers directly. All unfollow actions are performed manually by you, inside the official Instagram app.

Questions

This is an independent tool and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to Instagram or Meta.
